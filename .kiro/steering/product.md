# Studio Metavuz - Product Overview

Studio Metavuz is a professional music production studio website based in Lagos, Nigeria. The platform serves as a digital presence for a recording studio offering comprehensive music production services.

## Core Services
- **Beat Production**: Custom instrumentals across Afrobeat, Hip-Hop, RnB, Trap, Drill, and Dancehall
- **Recording Sessions**: High-quality vocal and live recording
- **Mixing & Mastering**: Professional audio post-production for radio and streaming platforms
- **Beat Remakes**: Industry beat recreation services

## Target Audience
- Musicians and artists seeking professional recording services
- Independent artists looking for beat production
- Music producers requiring mixing and mastering services
- Local Lagos music scene participants

## Key Features
- Online booking system for studio sessions
- Contact form for inquiries
- Studio gallery showcasing equipment and space
- Service portfolio with detailed offerings
- Professional branding with cyan/teal accent colors
- Mobile-responsive design with smooth animations

## Brand Identity
- Modern, professional aesthetic with dark theme
- Cyan (#06b6d4) as primary accent color
- Focus on "clean sound, hard beats, and a chill space to create"
- Emphasis on professionalism and quality in the Lagos music scene
